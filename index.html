How It's Made

From the earth itself.

01

Real Topographic Data

Every piece begins with actual elevation data pulled from the earth's surface — the same data cartographers use. If it exists on earth, we can map it.

02

Sculpted by Hand

The terrain is built layer by layer, ridge by ridge. Mary shapes each piece herself, bringing the contours off the data and into three dimensions.

03

Framed and Shipped

Finished white, set in a matte black frame with a small red heart marking your chosen spot. Ready to hang when it arrives at your door.

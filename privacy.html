Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gatekeep ("we", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. Gatekeep is operated by Jesiah Coates as a sole operator ("the operator"). Questions: thegalateacompany@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email and password — for sign-in. Passwords are never stored in plaintext; authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Display name and profile photo — chosen by you, shown to your friends and on guides you share.
  • Username (handle) — a unique identifier of the form name#1234, used to send and receive friend requests.

Location

  • Precise location (GPS) — collected only with your explicit permission. Used to: (a) calculate distance and walking time to your pinned places, (b) center the map on your location, and (c) let you drop a pin at your current spot.
  • Your location is processed on-device for distance calculations. We do not transmit or store your location history on our servers.

Content you create

  • Pins — name, optional note, optional address, optional photo, category, coordinates, and a timestamp.
  • Guides — name, optional photo, list of pins, list of collaborators, optional public/private setting.
  • Photos — when you upload a photo for a pin or guide, it is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Storage.
  • Friend connections — your list of friends and pending friend requests.

Usage information

  • Device identifiers — a Firebase-issued unique user ID used to associate your data with your account.
  • Screenshot events — for guides where the owner has enabled screenshot protection, the app records when a screenshot is taken (timestamp and the user who took it) so the owner is aware. No image content of the screenshot is ever captured or transmitted.
  • App diagnostics — basic crash and performance data via Apple App Store Connect and Expo. We do not run third-party advertising analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the app: sign you in, save your pins/guides, sync across devices, share content with friends you choose.
  • To improve reliability: diagnose crashes and performance issues.
  • To enforce safety: respond to abuse reports, enforce the Terms of Service.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising. We do not share your data with marketers.

3. Third-Party Services

Gatekeep relies on the following service providers to operate. Each processes your data only as needed to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage) — stores your account, pins, guides, and photos. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places API, Maps SDK) — used for place search, address lookup, and rendering the map. When you search, your query text and approximate location bias are sent to Google. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo — over-the-air updates and crash diagnostics. Subject to Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, TestFlight, in-app feedback systems. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing and Visibility

  • Private by default. Guides you create are visible only to you and to people you explicitly invite as editors.
  • Public guides. If you mark a guide as "Public", anyone with the link or anyone viewing your public profile can read its contents.
  • Friends. Your name, handle, and profile photo are visible to people you accept as friends. Guides you share with a friend are visible to that friend.
  • Pin attribution. When you add a pin to a shared guide, the other editors of that guide can see your name and profile photo as the contributor.

5. Your Rights and Controls

  • Access — you can view all the data you've created within the app at any time.
  • Edit and delete content — pins, guides, photos, and friend connections can be removed from within the app.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, your handle reservation, your guides, your pins, your photos, your friend list, and your entry from friend lists of other users.
  • Location permission — revoke at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Gatekeep.
  • Email us at thegalateacompany@gmail.com for any privacy request not covered above (data export, correction, complaint).

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our active systems immediately. Encrypted backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before being overwritten in the normal backup rotation, after which no copies remain.

7. Children

Gatekeep is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

Gatekeep is operated from the United States. By using the app you consent to your data being processed in the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your country. If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights (access, deletion, portability, objection) that you can exercise by contacting us at the email above.

9. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) for data in transit and rely on Google Firebase's security infrastructure for data at rest. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a breach affecting your data, we will notify you promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Gatekeep after a change means you accept the updated policy.
